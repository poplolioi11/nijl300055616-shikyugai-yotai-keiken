# 子宮外孕胎經驗（1857 年署記，千葉大學 86694） / Shikyūgai yōtai keiken [Case record of an extrauterine pregnancy]: Transcription Dataset

《子宮外孕胎經驗》是高橋一貫（桃源）於1857年署記的晚江戶漢文病例，記錄佐伯郡一位婦人的病程、數位醫者的診察、家屬的意見與照護結果。三宅春齡同年作序，記錄當時醫者對診斷證據及漢洋醫學的看法。底本為千葉大學附屬圖書館亥鼻分館86694，館目《宮外妊孕経験説并附録》。

Shikyūgai yōtai keiken is an 1857 Literary Chinese case record signed by 高橋一貫桃源, with a Literary Chinese preface by 三宅春齡. The record follows a patient in Saeki District through a prolonged illness, consultations, decisions by the family, and the outcome of care. This transcription follows Chiba University Library's Inohana collection, shelfmark 86694, catalogued as Kyūgai ninyō keikensetsu narabi ni furoku.

## V0.1.0

逐欄錄第3幅左至第6幅左的序、完整題名病例與兩篇署記，另記第1幅攝影指標、第2幅封題、第3幅寄贈附札及第7幅另附和文的篇界。AI輔助初錄與原圖逐幅定位，已知疑讀保留於相應欄位。

Transcribes the Literary Chinese preface and complete separately titled case on scans 3–6, with the cover, imaging target, donation note, and the beginning of the separate Japanese appendix located as source features. AI-assisted initial transcription with image references and explicitly marked doubtful readings.

## 方法與覆核 / Methods and review

由 AI 依固定館方影像逐欄讀字，保留原字、版面與疑讀。保留逐欄讀序、原寫字形、重文、原筆刪記、印識及部分可定位的訓點；序文旁寫小訓仍待逐字覆核。人類全文雙校、專家審定、同儕審查及準確率量測尚未進行。實際模型識別與使用量未知。此資料公開供比對與引用，處理程式及影像另存。

An AI assistant transcribed the fixed source images column by column, preserving source forms, layout and uncertain readings. Follows the column order and preserves source forms, iteration signs, deletions, seals and the clearly locatable reading marks. Some small side glosses in the preface remain to be read. Human proofreading, expert review, peer review and accuracy measurement are pending. The actual model identifiers and usage are unknown.
